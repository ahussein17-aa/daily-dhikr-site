Content corrections

The adhkar in Daily Dhikr are drawn from Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Abu Dawud, at-Tirmidhi and Hisn al-Muslim. If you spot an error in the Arabic, transliteration, translation or reference, please email with the dhikr and its category — corrections are taken seriously and shipped in updates.

Common questions

Does the app need an internet connection? No. All content is included with the app and everything works offline.

How do I add the widget? Long-press your home screen, tap the + button, and search for "Daily Dhikr" to add the Dhikr Streak widget in small or medium size.

My reminders aren't arriving. Check that reminders are on in the app's Settings, then check Settings → Notifications → Daily Dhikr on your iPhone, and make sure Focus modes allow it.

How is my streak counted? Complete at least one session on a day to keep your streak going. Your progress is stored only on your device — see the privacy policy.
