Daily Dhikr — Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Daily Dhikr collects no data. None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party services. It works entirely offline.

What the app stores

Everything you do in Daily Dhikr stays on your device. Your daily progress, streak, reminder times, Arabic font and appearance settings are stored in the app's local storage on your iPhone, shared only with the app's own home screen widget. Nothing is transmitted anywhere — the app makes no network connections at all.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled as local notifications on your device by iOS. No notification service or server is involved.

Device backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or your computer, that backup includes what Daily Dhikr stored, so your progress comes back when you restore. That backup belongs to your Apple account; Daily Dhikr never sees it.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on the device. The only other copy is in your own device backup, if you use one, and you manage that in your iPhone's settings. Daily Dhikr holds nothing anywhere else.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above — for example, by adding an online feature — this policy will be updated first, and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.
